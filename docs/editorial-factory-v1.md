# Editorial Factory V1\n\nThe editorial control plane turns research feeds into reviewable faceless-video packages.\n\n## Pipeline\n\n`idea -> researched -> scripted -> generated -> review -> approved -> uploaded_private -> public_approved -> published -> measured -> learning`\n\nThere are two independent human gates:\n\n1. **Production gate** — the generated video/package must pass QA and a human must approve it before private upload.\n2. **Public gate** — a second explicit human approval is required after private upload. The API cannot mark a video public without that state.\n\n## Editorial Agent\n\n`app/editorial/agent.py` ranks research candidates with a transparent weighted score:\n\n| Dimension | Weight |\n|---|---:|\n| Freshness | 20 |\n| Audience relevance | 20 |\n| Novelty | 15 |\n| Evidence strength | 15 |\n| Visual potential | 15 |\n| Hook potential | 10 |\n| Production feasibility | 5 |\n\nThis is deliberately deterministic in V1. The scoring is inspectable and can later be replaced or augmented with learned performance signals.\n\n## Content Engine\n\n`app/editorial/content_engine.py` creates a structured package:\n\n- original 8–14 minute script target\n- unique summary at the top of the description\n- five title variants\n- tags\n- estimated chapters\n- visual beats and search terms\n- thumbnail brief\n- source/evidence notes\n- synthetic-media flag\n- QA report\n\nThe script generator reuses the existing MoneyPrinterTurbo LLM provider configuration instead of adding another model dependency.\n\n## QA\n\nThe QA gate blocks:\n\n- missing title\n- invalid title length\n- missing/too-short/too-long script\n- missing description\n- missing unique summary in the first lines\n- bracket placeholders\n- insufficient research depth\n- invalid source URLs\n- missing metadata tags\n- prohibited guaranteed-return or individualized buy/sell language on Capital Signal\n\nCapital Signal requires at least three research sources per package.\n\n## API\n\nExisting:\n\n- `GET /api/v1/editorial/channels`\n- `GET /api/v1/editorial/episodes`\n- `GET /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}`\n- `GET /api/v1/editorial/insights`
- `GET /api/v1/editorial/dashboard`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/research/collect`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/research`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/script`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/generate`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/sync`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/review`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/publish`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/metrics`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/public-approve`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/mark-public`\n\nNew V1:\n\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/opportunities?channel=nexbrain`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/opportunities?channel=capital-signal`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/opportunity`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/package`\n- `POST /api/v1/editorial/episodes/{episode_id}/qa`\n\n## n8n\n\n`automation/n8n/editorial-factory-v1.json` contains the orchestration blueprint.\n\nExpected environment variables in n8n:\n\n- `MONEYTUBE_BASE_URL`\n- `MONEYTUBE_API_KEY`\n\nThe workflow creates one candidate per pilot channel per daily run, researches it, generates the package, runs QA, renders the video through the existing engine, polls until the render reaches review or failure, and then stops at the human review gate. It does **not** upload or publish automatically.\n\n## Pilot queue\n\n`app/editorial/pilot_queue.json` contains 10 candidate topics for Nexbrain and 10 for Capital Signal. These are candidate angles, not permanent factual claims. Research is re-collected before scripting.\n\n## Operating rule\n\nNo public auto-publish is enabled in V1. The intended production loop is:\n\n**research -> score -> package -> QA -> human review -> render -> human approval -> private upload -> final public approval -> publish -> measure -> learn.**\n
